Reaching rules

Sail selection (from the manual)
A-sail: very light air TWA >90 deg; normal winds deploy at TWA 105 deg; 18 kts+ only if TWA >120 deg. Spin (.6oz) is primary reaching/running sail 7-18 kts.
Symmetric crossover (from the ORC cert)
On the polar the symmetric spinnaker only beats the headsail at TWA ~110 deg and deeper — at a true beam reach (90 deg) white sails are faster. The asymmetric A-sail carries higher than this, which is why the manual flies it above 90 deg. Rule of thumb: symmetric below 110 deg is slow; heat up to the kite's angle or stay on white sails.
Backstay rule
Released completely for reaching or running -- no half measures.
